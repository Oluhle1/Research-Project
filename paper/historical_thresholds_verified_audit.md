|---|---:|

Exact effective-date sentence:

> The increase will be effected on 01 April 2014 with a further adjustment of R10 in October 2014 for the Child Support Grant and Grant-in-Aid respectively.

The older-person's October column is blank; the additional October adjustment is expressly for child support and grant-in-aid. This supports retaining R1,350 as the standard monthly maximum for the study's October 2014–March 2015 period.

The accompanying amendment, PDF2 / Gazette4, states:

> Words underlined with a solid line indicates insertions in the existing regulations.

PDF3 / Gazette5, Schedule paragraph2:

> Paragraph 2 of Annexure A is hereby substituted for the following paragraph:

The marked formula is:

> D = [1,4A − 0,4B] 1,6A − 0,4B

The image shows the bracketed term in bold as deleted and the 1.6 term underlined as inserted. The resulting operative formula is **D = 1.6A − 0.4B**. This is an enacted substitution, not two simultaneous formulas or draft text.

Same page, substituted Annexure A(2)(i)–(iii):

> (i) A = the maximum social grant payable per annum as approved;

> (ii) B = the annual income of the applicant in accordance with regulation 19, which shall include the income from assets as determined in regulation 19(3), after permissible deductions contemplated in regulation 20 are effected;

## Original minimum, joint income and desertion provisions

R898/2008, Annexure A(5), physical/printed page75:

> No grant amounting to less than R100 per month is payable.

The monthly minimum payable amount corresponds to **R1,200 per annum**. The wording excludes amounts below R100; it does not itself exclude equality or require smaller calculated amounts to be rounded up.

Regulation19(1), page41:

> For the purposes of determining means, in respect of social assistance, except for a grant in-aid and the foster child grant, the income of the applicant is deemed to be the annual income for an applicant not in a spousal relationship, or half the annual income of the applicant and his or her spouse, where the applicant is in a spousal relationship, and “income” means—

Regulation18(2), page40:

> The income of an applicant and his or her spouse must be taken into account irrespective of whether the couple is married in or out of community of property under the Marriage Act, 1961 or any provisions contained in an ante-nuptial contract.

Regulation18(3), same page:

> The Agency must, if it is satisfied that an applicant has been deserted by his or her spouse for a continuous period of at least three months, and the spouse is unwilling to support the applicant, grant approval for the means of that spouse not to be taken into consideration when determining the means of the applicant.

Annexure A(3)–(4), page75, likewise describes continuous desertion for at least three months, unwillingness to support and Agency approval to disregard spouse means. A survey category of separated does not establish those facts or that approval. Regulation1's page15 spouse definition links spouse/partner status to the listed Marriage Act, customary-marriage, Civil Union Act and religious bases; survey cohabitation alone does not authenticate that classification.

The directly supplied 2014 amendment substitutes Annexure A(2), not paragraph5 or regulations18–20. R621 likewise makes no substitution of those OPG provisions. This is original-source support for the research calculation, without an exhaustive line-by-line consolidation claim for every other historical clause.

## R621: no July 2015 OPG breakpoint

R621, PDF16 / Gazette19, amendment paragraph18:

> These regulations will come into effect on the date of publication of this notice.

The publication date is **21 July 2015**. Complete inspection finds changes to regulation1 definitions, regulations7, 9, 14–16, 28(3), 32–33, and insertion of37A. There is **no amendment of regulations18, 19 or20, the spouse definition, or Annexure A**. Paragraph7 inserts “temporary safe care” after “spouse”; it does not replace “spouse”. The assets/income declaration in regulation15 is expressly for an SRD application, not an OPG means substitution.

Thus R621 creates **no July 2015 OPG formula/means breakpoint**. It does not remove dependent-child/ex-gratia income or add the later asset proviso. Those changes must not be attributed to this notice.

## Four standard-rate research ceilings

Use annual amounts. At the R100 monthly payment boundary, D = R1,200 and:

`B = (1.6A − 1,200) / 0.4 = 4A − 3,000`.

For a spousal relationship, B is half the joint annual income, so the corresponding joint total is twice the individual boundary.

| Interview period | Standard monthly maximum | Annual A | Single annual ceiling | Joint annual couple ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| October 2014–March 2015 | R1,350 | R16,200 | **R61,800** | **R123,600** |
| April–October 2015 | R1,410 | R16,920 | **R64,680** | **R129,360** |

These are **formula-derived research ceilings**, not income-threshold figures printed as such in the two rate tables. The primary implementation includes equality at these boundaries. The source minimum does not exclude an exact R100 payment, but crossing this numerical test alone does not establish all legal conditions.

Both rate notices expressly publish **+R20 monthly at age75+**. Using the standard 60–74 maximum in the primary screen at all ages is therefore a stated **research choice**, not the complete age-specific statutory maximum. The existing **S1c age-75-supplement sensitivity remains unchanged**. No new scenario run or refit was performed. Adding the supplement to A in the same calculation raises an individual annual boundary by R960; that arithmetic does not change the primary ceilings above.

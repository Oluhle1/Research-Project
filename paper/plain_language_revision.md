# Plain-language paper revision

The paper now uses the single author's “I” for research actions and “you” when guiding readers through results or the app. I replaced dense noun phrases with actions, split packed explanations, and defined necessary technical terms in parentheses at their first explanation. I also simplified headings, table wording, figure captions and diagram labels.

The title and the published titles in the bibliography remain unchanged. The author-confirmed module code is **APRE402**. The regenerated paper contains **eight US Letter pages, nine tables and four figures**, including its references.

## What changed

- “Administrative-burden theory motivates candidate predictors” now explains that I used the theory to choose survey characteristics. Learning, compliance and psychological costs have direct explanations.
- Technical terms such as AUC, average precision, Brier score, household-held-out predictions, odds ratios and calibration have parenthetical explanations. “Cohort,” “prevalence,” “parity,” “serialization” and “metrics” usually become ordinary words.
- The app's diagram says the saved model was trained on the included study group. It does not imply that every survey adult entered the model.
- Separate explanations preserve apparent screening, reported receipt, predicted non-receipt and legal entitlement.

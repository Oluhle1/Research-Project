- DUT Research Ethics Policy, last amended 21 February 2023, with the updated
  policy approved on 15 June 2023. Clause 3.1 covers postgraduate research
  students and undergraduate students who are undertaking research. Clause 3.2
  requires ethical consideration where research collects data on individuals.
  Clause 3.3 gives DUT-IREC a central approval role and, where that route is
  not used, requires a formal documented faculty or departmental procedure.
  Clause 6.2 states that DUT-IREC allocates the ethics clearance number before
  the study commences. Clause 7 includes indirect access to personal
  information among work that requires ethical assessment. Clause 7.1 lists the
  aged among groups that may be particularly vulnerable.
- DUT Research Ethics Terms of Reference, dated 3 July 2023. DUT-IREC is
  registered with the National Health Research Ethics Council. A faculty
  research ethics committee reviews category 2 proposals and cannot issue an
  ethics clearance number. Category 2 proposals go to DUT-IREC for the quality
  check and the clearance number. DUT-IREC reviews proposals that require
  ethical clearance in categories 2 and 3.
- DUT Postgraduate Student Guide 2026. Its proposal route sends ethics
  categories 1, 2 and 3 to IREC after Faculty Research Committee approval. That
  guide is not an APRE402 instruction and is not this project's determination.
- Older DUT classification guideline. Category 1 is exempt from IREC review
  only after a Faculty Research Committee or Faculty Research Ethics Committee
  classifies it. That route still requires faculty review, an ethics checklist,
  and the stated gatekeeper and consent conditions. The guideline says IREC
  does not issue a clearance certificate for a Category 1 decision, and, in
  that paragraph, that retrospective ethics clearance cannot be granted.
  Category 1 examples include public-domain material and existing collections
  that contain only non-identifiable data. The same guideline places access to
  records containing personal or sensitive information, and research involving
  the elderly, at category 2 or above. This revision does not treat LCS
  microdata as public-domain or non-identifiable, and it does not assign a
  category.
